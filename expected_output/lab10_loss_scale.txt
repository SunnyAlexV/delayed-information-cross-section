====================================================================================

identical -> True.  QLIKE depends on the

Squared error on LOG variance has no such property and is not in the family.

====================================================================================
3.  THE delta = 0 ROW, WHICH CHANGES SIGN
====================================================================================
On log-scale squared error the cross-section is slightly WORSE at zero delay,
and the paper leans on that row when discussing Korkusuz and Jayawardena.
On the robust variance-scale loss the sign flips.  Either way the question is
whether the difference is distinguishable from zero, so test it.

      loss     S own   S cross   difference          95% interval
   sq(log)    0.4987    0.4884      -0.0103     [-0.0192,-0.0016]  <- excludes zero
     QLIKE    0.5721    0.5578      -0.0142     [-0.0292,-0.0023]  <- excludes zero
  MSE(var)    0.3012    0.3084      +0.0071     [-0.0470,+0.0280]

A claim that rests on a row whose SIGN depends on the scale is a claim the
paper should not make.  Whatever survives here is what it may say.

The robust loss is the one to believe if they disagree.  If the rates in
part 1 sit on top of each other, the headline survives the correction and
the paper owes the reader a sentence naming which loss is robust, not a
new set of numbers.
