Read the GW column, not dR2 and not 'net'.  'net' is a difference of two
estimated quantities carrying no interval, which is the trap lab07
documented; it is printed because the cost of flexibility is the mechanism,
but no claim rests on its sign.

What this supports, if the GW column holds: the redundancy of the foreign
block given implied volatility is not an artefact of the linear estimator.
It survives a quadratic global factor, an interaction between that factor
and implied volatility, and a random-feature approximation to an RBF kernel
at two capacities and under a penalty grid wide enough to regularise it.

What it cannot support: that no non-linear model could ever find anything.
A learned first layer was not tested, and this sample could not pay for
one.  That is the same weaker claim lab09 draws, now covering the case
Section 7 actually rests on.
