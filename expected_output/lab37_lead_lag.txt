A2.  BEFORE READING ANY OF THAT AS DIRECTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A row of this panel is not an instant.  The target closes at 21:00 UTC and a
peer closes hours EARLIER on the same row, so the two directions are measured
over different amounts of elapsed time:

    peer(t-1) -> target(t)   takes  24 + c  hours
    target(t-1) -> peer(t)   takes  24 - c  hours

where c is how many hours before the target's close that peer's close falls.
For a persistent series the shorter gap gives the higher correlation, so a
NEGATIVE asymmetry is exactly what the clock predicts even if nothing leads
anything.  The clock's prediction is testable: the size of the asymmetry
should track 2c across peers.  If it does, this table measures the clock.

======================================================================================================
VERDICT
======================================================================================================
  raw asymmetry: 0 of 7 peers lead, 7 lag - but it tracks the trading
  clock at rho = +0.933, so direction is NOT established by part A.
  foreign staleness hurts in 25 of 30 cells, 5 of 6 at a single day; one stale foreign
  day costs 0.13 to 1.44 days of domestic freshness, most where the mark is oldest.
  lagged factor worse than contemporaneous in 26 of 30 cells; factor history adds at 2 of 6 delays.

  THE PRACTICAL FINDING, and it is a limitation neither paper states: the
  foreign block's value is largely its FRESHNESS.  One day of foreign
  staleness costs real skill at almost every delay.  Both papers were
  tested on a same-day foreign feed and never say they assume one; a desk
  reading yesterday's foreign closes gets materially less than the tables
  in those papers promise, and the size of the shortfall is in B2.
  On direction: the raw asymmetry is the clock, but yesterday's factor
  still adds to today's at [3, 5] - short delays only.  That is a small
  amount of genuine dynamics sitting inside an otherwise contemporaneous
  story, and it is where a richer specification would have to start.
