A.  WHICH HALF CARRIES THE RECOVERY?
================================================================================================
OWN is the domestic-only model.  PC1 adds the global component alone; REST
adds components two through seven, which are orthogonal to it by construction.
The last column tests PC1 against REST by Giacomini-White; positive favours
the global component.

  The recovery is the global component.  Orthogonalised against it, the
  foreign block has nothing left that this design can detect, so what the
  paper calls breadth is one common factor observed while the domestic
  series is stale - not bilateral diffusion between markets.  The
  substitution rate is unaffected; what changes is the name of the
  mechanism, and the referee is right that the paper should give it.
