  ratio                                  3.7%

A median over 252 days moves slowly, so the two targets are close. Close
is not the same as irrelevant, and the correlation above is the reason to
check: the drift is not independent of what is being predicted.

If those two lines agree, the median's five-day drift is not doing any
work and the paper may keep its definition, reporting this run as the
