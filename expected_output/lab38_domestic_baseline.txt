==================================================================================================
D.  WHICH OF THE TWO DOES IT, AND IS THE GAIN LEGITIMATE?
==================================================================================================
+MEAS carries two features and they are not the same kind of thing. One is a
second realised measure, a 22-day Yang-Zhang, which is simply less noisy than
the 5-day one. The other is log M, the LEVEL of the trailing median, which
every model in the paper is blind to because the normalisation divides it out.
The distinction matters: the target is log(variance / M at t+5), so a model
that sees log M at t-delta can partly reconstruct the target's own denominator,
M being slow. That is admissible - every feature is dated t-delta - but it is
a fact about how the target is built as much as about volatility, and it would
be wrong to report it as though the two features were interchangeable.
