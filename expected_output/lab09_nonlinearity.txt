The 'net of cost' column is NOT read as a gain anywhere above, and the
reason is the one lab07 had to learn: it is a difference of two estimated
quantities and carries no interval here, so a positive entry sitting beside
a dR2 of -0.047 and a GW statistic of -3.92 is noise in the cost estimate,
not a finding.  What the GW column supports is all that is claimed:

  SQ is actively worse.  Squaring every input doubles the parameter count
  and buys nothing, which is the ordinary overfitting story.
  FACTOR is indistinguishable from LIN - no statistic reaches 1.96 in
  either direction.  Letting the global state enter quadratically and
  interact with the domestic state changes nothing measurable.

A non-linear map that cannot clear its own estimation cost is not evidence
that the world is linear.  It is evidence that this sample cannot pay for
the extra parameters.  That is the claim the paper makes, and it is weaker
than 'linearity is sufficient' on purpose.
