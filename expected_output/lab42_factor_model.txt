3.  READING, NOT A PREDICTION: R(delta) RISES TOWARDS S_cross / S_own(0)
============================================================================================
This part used to call itself a prediction and the verdict used to count
it as one, which Section S26 of the appendix explicitly retracts: as
S_own(delta) goes to zero the ceiling S_cross(55)/S_own(0) and the
measured R(55) = [S_cross(55)-S_own(55)]/[S_own(0)-S_own(55)] become the
same expression in the same two numbers, so they MUST agree and the gap
between them is the size of S_own(55) rather than the accuracy of a
forecast.  It is reported as the reading the model gives the plateau, and
it is not counted as a test; the package must not assert a confirmation
the paper withdraws.

============================================================================================
VERDICT
============================================================================================
  3 of 3 testable predictions hold; part 3 is a reading and is
  not counted, for the reason it states.
  The decay of a stale forecaster's skill is geometric, and the rate of
  decay implies a persistence that matches the factor's own. The delay
  curve is the factor's memory, measured through a forecast.
  The ceiling is where the curve goes, and that is arithmetic rather
  than corroboration: the two are the same two numbers once S_own has
  gone. What the model earns is the READING - R(delta) is a factor
  share and the level it flattens at is that share - and not credit
  for predicting a number it is algebraically tied to.
