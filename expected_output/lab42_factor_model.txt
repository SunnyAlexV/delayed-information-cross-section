============================================================================================
VERDICT
============================================================================================
  4 of 4 predictions hold.
  The decay of a stale forecaster's skill is geometric, and the rate of
  decay implies a persistence that matches the factor's own. The delay
  curve is the factor's memory, measured through a forecast.
  The ceiling the model predicts from two independently measured
  quantities is where the curve actually goes. R(delta) is not an
  arbitrary rising curve; it is a factor-share, and the paper can say
  what it converges to and why.
