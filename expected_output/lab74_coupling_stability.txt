================================================================================================
E.  THE CONTRAST, AND WHAT IT LICENSES
================================================================================================
Two marginal sets failing to overlap is not a test of a difference,
which is why the difference itself is resampled here, in blocks of
80 days with 2000 replications, both
buckets resampled inside the same replication.

 delta   R stable  R unstable   difference     95% block bootstrap
     5        57%         59%          -2%       [   -44%,   +26%]
    13        65%         71%          -5%       [   -36%,   +14%]
    21        65%         78%         -12%       [   -43%,    +8%]
    55        70%         80%         -10%       [   -34%,   +11%]

  the stable-minus-unstable difference excludes zero at 0 of 4 delays,
  and is negative at 4 of 4.
  The limitation predicts a POSITIVE difference: the rate should be
  lower where the structure has just reorganised.

  No delay separates the two states on this measure.  That is a
  failure to detect, not a demonstration that stability does not
  matter: the unstable bucket is a third of the sample and the
  sets below are wide.

  Fieller sets for the two extreme buckets at the longest delay,
  which is where the rate is best identified:
       stable  R =   70%     interval   [48%, 87%]
