  A printed statistic at the threshold cannot be classified from
  printed text, because the value behind it may lie either side.
  lab68 reads the stored loss differentials rather than this table
  and is the instrument the paper quotes for the count; where the
  two disagree, they disagree by these cells and lab68 is right.

  One further limit of this file, and it is not small. The
  corrections above are computed from NORMAL p-values, and lab68
  measures the null these statistics actually have under a block
  resampling of the same evaluation days: the marginal rule rejects
  about 9% of the time rather than 5%. A Benjamini-Hochberg or
  Bonferroni threshold applied to p-values from the wrong reference
  distribution is not conservative in a known direction, so the
  survivors below are reported as what this rule returns and not as
  cells that have survived a correction. Section S37 says which
  instrument each number in Table S30 comes from.

  In no case does this touch the headline, which is one pre-specified
  comparison with its own Reality Check and SPA test in lab34.
