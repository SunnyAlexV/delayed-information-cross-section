================================================================================
IS THE FOREIGN BLOCK HARMFUL, OR MERELY REDUNDANT?
================================================================================
The incremental column above is NEGATIVE.  Read literally that says
foreign closes damage a forecast that already has implied volatility,
which would be a strange thing for genuine information to do.  lab07
measured the alternative: seven extra regressors cost about a point of
R-squared whether or not they help.  Subtract that and the question
becomes whether the foreign block's content given implied volatility is
negative or simply zero - a difference the paper has to get right.

Same instrument as lab07: replace the seven foreign series with AR(1)
surrogates matched on persistence and variance, so they carry no
information by construction.  What remains is the bill.

Gross gets an interval before it gets a label, for the reason lab07
gives: it is a difference of two estimated quantities.
