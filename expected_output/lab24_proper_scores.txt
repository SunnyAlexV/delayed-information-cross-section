A.  ACCURACY, RANKING, AND PROBABILITY QUALITY
========================================================================================
acc and AUC are available for every forecaster: the persistence rules
are ranked by the margin a = log(v/M) whose sign IS the rule.  Brier
and log score need a probability, which a hard rule does not emit;
those cells are n/a by construction, not by omission.

Two AUC columns, and the distance between them is the referee's point
made into a measurement.  AUC(margin) ranks days by the continuous
decision variable a forecaster HAS; AUC(label) ranks by the 0/1 it
actually EMITS, and for a binary forecast it equals balanced accuracy.
A rule that throws away its margin gives up the gap between them.

  label ranks 0.014 HIGHER than the margin, which is possible
  because a sign is not a monotone summary of a margin once the margin's
  ordering stops tracking the outcome.  Either way the two differ, and the
  note's accuracy table cannot see the difference.

  A caution on the base-rate row, which reads below chance.  The recursive
  base rate is a running mean of resolved labels: it is nearly constant, so
  its ranking is decided by slow drift rather than by anything it knows.
  Its AUC is 0.377; ranking by calendar time alone gives 0.504.
  Those do NOT agree, so the drift is not a simple calendar trend - the
  running mean rises after clusters of ones, and the target mean-reverts
  against it.  We report the mechanism as unresolved rather than guess.
  In either case the row is not a chance benchmark and should not be read as
  one; chance for every other row is 0.500 by construction.
