================================================================================================
VERDICT
================================================================================================
  At the S&P's unverifiable join all three instruments sit inside their
  own null distributions: level 70th percentile, alignment 4th,
  range 25th. The relationship between the S&P's own OHLC and an
  exchange's index of the same underlying does not break across that
  date; the same-day return correlation does not move; and the two
  volatility estimators inside the file keep their ratio.

  The limitation does not vanish, it acquires a size. A one-session
  misalignment and a spliced block are both caught, and so is an
  intraday range mis-scaled by 25%. Section 11 can now say what
  was checked and how large a fault would have had to be, instead of
  saying that comparison was impossible.
