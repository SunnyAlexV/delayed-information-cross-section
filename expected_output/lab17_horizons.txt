====================================================================================
A.  DOES THE TARGET OVERLAP THE FEATURES, AND BY HOW MUCH?
====================================================================================
The Yang-Zhang proxy spans five days, so for h < 5 the outcome window and
the domestic feature's own window share days.  Nothing is leaked - the
outcome is still in the future - but a forecaster at h = 1 is predicting an
object they can largely already see, which inflates every arm at once.

   h  days shared with sigma^2_t    corr(a_t, a_t+h)
   1                           4              0.9532
   5                           0              0.6917
  10                           0              0.5764
  21                           0              0.4172

  five target days are already inside the domestic feature, so both arms score
  high and the denominator of the rate - how much a delay costs a domestic-only
  forecaster - is small.  A rate computed against a small denominator is the
