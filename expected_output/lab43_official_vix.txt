A.  THE TWO SOURCES
============================================================================================
  official (Cboe)   9272 rows  1990-01-02 to 2026-09-14  ['VIX_History.csv']
  retail export     6762 rows  2000-01-03 to 2026-09-11  ['CBOE_Volatility_Index_Historical_Data.csv', 'CBOE_Volatility_Index_Historical_Data_1.csv']

Only the close is used: the implied-volatility decision variable is the
log of the level over its own trailing 252-day median, so the official
file's open, high and low are read and discarded. That matters, because
the official file has no intraday range before June 2004 - Cboe published
closes only - and a range-based estimator would have inherited the gap.

============================================================================================
VERDICT
============================================================================================
  Cboe's own series and the retail export agree to the cent on
  99.91% of 6747 shared rows, and the 2 rows outside
  the project's 0.5% tolerance are shortened sessions. The export
  additionally carries 15 rows on days United States equities were
  shut, which is a fabrication rather than a discrepancy - but every one
  falls outside the panel, so no model was ever shown one. Substituting
  the official file moves no cell of Section 8 by as much as a thousandth
  of R-squared. The paper now uses Cboe's file, and this lab is the
  reason the change can be described as housekeeping rather than
  asserted to be.
