2. SIMULTANEOUS BANDS ACROSS THE DELTA GRID
==================================================================================
Ten pointwise 95% intervals will breach somewhere by chance.  We bootstrap
the whole delta-vector jointly and take the sup-t critical value.

sup-t critical value 2.53  (pointwise 95% would use 1.96)
  bootstrap block 80 days, 3000 draws, 10 delays,
  studentised by a HAC standard error at the same 80 lags - one
  length for the kernel and the resampling, and it is block_for(55),
  the longest dependence in the vector this band covers
  distribution of the sup-t statistic: median 1.28, 90th 2.18, 95th 2.53, 99th 3.18
  Bonferroni would use 2.81 for 10 delays at 5%
  the bootstrap value is below it, which is expected: the delays share
  overlapping windows of one series, so treating them as independent
  overstates the correction.
