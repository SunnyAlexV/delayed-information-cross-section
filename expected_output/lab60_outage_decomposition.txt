============================================================================================
A.  THE TWO PIECES OF WHAT DELTA COSTS
============================================================================================
Every arm shares the features, the estimator, the refit schedule and the
test days. The FEATURE-ONLY arm is infeasible under this paper's own
information set - training to t - h needs the stretch of the target's own
series the delay withholds - and exists to price one effect, never as a
forecast this paper reports.

  the estimation vintage accounts for 0.4% to 2.7% of what the delay costs
  So the outage is mostly a stale INPUT. Freezing the coefficients,
  the penalty and the training window costs a minor share of the
  damage at every delay, and R(delta) divides by a denominator that
  is predominantly information rather than estimation. The naming
  matters and the measurement does not change much.

============================================================================================
B.  DOES THE SUBSTITUTION RATE CARE WHICH ONE IT IS?
============================================================================================
R(delta) recomputed inside the feature-only arm, with the cross-sectional
model given the same cut as the domestic one so the comparison stays
internal. If the two rates agree, the headline is a statement about
stale information and not about frozen estimation.

 delta   R outage  R feature-only   difference

    13      63.7%           64.8%        -1.1%

    55      71.6%           74.4%        -2.8%

  largest difference between the two rates: 2.8%
  The rate does not distinguish the two designs. Whatever delta is
  called, the share of the damage that breadth repairs is the same,
  so the naming is a matter of accuracy rather than of result.

============================================================================================
VERDICT
============================================================================================
  The design freezes the domestic information system for delta days and
  the paper now names it that way. This file prices the part of that
  freeze which is not the input, so a reader can see how much of
  R(delta)'s denominator is information and how much is estimation.
