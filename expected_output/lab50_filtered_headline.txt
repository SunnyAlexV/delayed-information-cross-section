VERDICT
================================================================================================
  Inside the estimator the paper declined, the headline is LARGER:
  75.4% against 71.6% at eleven weeks, a paired +3.8% [+0.8%, +7.7%],
  and the mark it is worth reads 3.7 days old rather than 4.6.

  So the estimator limitation does not run the way a limitation
  usually does. The filter improves the model that carries the
  cross-section and has nothing to add to the control, so it raises
  the numerator and leaves the denominator alone. The paper is not
  protecting a large number with a weak estimator. It is quoting a
  smaller number than the better estimator would give it, for the
  transparency of a fixed feature set, and this file prices that
  choice in the paper's own units instead of in R-squared.

  What this does not do is recompute the conditionals, the regime
  ladder, the five markets or the horse race inside the filter. That is
  the different paper Section 11 declines to write, and it is still
  declined. What was owed was the headline, and the headline is here.
