====================================================================================================
VERDICT
====================================================================================================
  Nine-day implied volatility is the better instrument at 5 of
  5 delays, which is the referee's point and it is correct.
  It does not change the conclusion: the foreign cross-section still
  adds nothing detectable on top of it. The horizon mismatch made the
  paper's implied-volatility control WEAKER than it should have been,
  and redundancy held even against the weaker control.

  The window is the standing caveat: it begins after 2008 and cannot
  speak to the episode where delay is most expensive. Table 8 remains the
  paper's headline for that reason, and this lab is the sharper test on
  the sample that exists.
