C.  IS ANY DIFFERENCE TIME, OR IS IT STRESS COMPOSITION?
================================================================================================
Section 8.1 showed the rate is higher in stress, and the halves do not
contain equal amounts of it. So compare halves WITHIN a VIX tercile: days
that were equally frightening, differing only in when they happened.

    VIX files: ['VIX_History.csv']
VIX terciles at t-1: calm <= 15.4, middle, stressed > 20.7

The two halves are disjoint samples, so each rate is bootstrapped
separately and the draws are differenced. A calm-market rate is poorly
determined by construction - there is little delay damage in a calm market,
so the ratio's denominator is small - and a large point difference there can
easily be noise. The interval, not the gap, is what says which.

================================================================================================
D.  THE RATE THE PAPER SHOULD ACTUALLY QUOTE
================================================================================================
Parts A to C say the unconditional rate is an average over states in which
the mechanism works very differently, and over an era in which one of those
states has been getting quieter. A number worth quoting has to name its
state. These are pooled over the whole window, by VIX tercile, with the
whole-ratio block bootstrap the paper uses everywhere else.

================================================================================================
VERDICT
================================================================================================
  The halves' intervals overlap everywhere, but the rolling path drifts
  by more than ten points a decade at [5, 13, 21, 55]. The drift is within
  sampling noise at the half-sample level and visible in the path; both
  facts belong in any statement about stability.
  Within a VIX tercile the halves differ by up to 52 points,
  and by more than fifteen in 7 of the 12 cells, so the
  differences are not small in magnitude.
  But not one of those differences is significant: every cell's interval
  covers zero, so what difference there is between eras is mostly which
  days each era contained, not a change in how the cross-section works.
