and every number in both papers would be optimistic by that much.

  cells where a larger embargo significantly reduces skill: 0 of 18

  Adding an embargo on top of the purge costs nothing detectable at any
  delay. The gap this project already leaves is sufficient, and the
  walk-forward design was purged correctly before anyone called it that.
