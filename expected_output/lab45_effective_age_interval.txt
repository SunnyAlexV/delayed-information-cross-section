================================================================================================
VERDICT
================================================================================================
  The effective age is 4.6 days with a 95% interval of [2.5, 8.4].
  Every value in that interval is a small fraction of the 55 days of
  staleness the cross-section is repairing, so the headline is a finding
  and not an artefact of reading one point off a curve. It should be
  quoted with the interval, which is what this lab exists to supply.

  A single day of foreign staleness costs 2.1 days of domestic
  freshness, interval [1.4, 3.3], which excludes zero. Section 9.3's
  exchange rate is a measured difference and not a direction, and it is
  now quotable in the unit the section is written in. The two marginal
  intervals in part B overlap, which is the ordinary behaviour of
  marginal intervals and is not evidence against a paired difference.
