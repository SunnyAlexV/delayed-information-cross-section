================================================================================================
B.  WHAT THE PARAMETERS ARE WORTH KNOWING
================================================================================================
Parameter sets are drawn by simulating the fitted model over the
estimation block and refitting, then the whole panel is re-filtered
with each draw.  The spread of the reconstruction across those draws
is the part of the uncertainty the frozen filter does not report.

A moving-block resample of the block itself will not do here, and
the reason is worth stating: the factor is persistent, the joins
between blocks break that persistence, and the refit comes back with
phi biased down and q biased up - far enough that the band excludes
its own point estimate, which is a sign the instrument is wrong
rather than a finding about the parameter.

  200 of 200 draws produced a fit

  The draws are refitted the way the data were, so they carry the
  estimator's own attenuation: a factor extracted from noisy series
  is a noisy factor, and an AR(1) coefficient fitted to it comes back
  low.  The raw quantiles therefore sit below the point estimate and
  are not a band for it.  What the draws measure honestly is the
  SPREAD, so the band below is recentred on the point estimate and
  the raw quantiles are printed beside it rather than hidden.

VERDICT
================================================================================================
  With parameters a forecaster could have had, the filter is still the better
  cross-sectional model at eleven weeks: 0.3788 against the ridge's 0.3729.
  The rate at eleven weeks reads 72.6% under live parameters against 75.3% frozen
  and 71.5% in the published specification.
  The paired difference against the ridge does not exclude zero,
  so the filtered specification is not separated from the

  the reconstruction, and the innovation diagnostics above say
  whether even that band is an honest floor.
