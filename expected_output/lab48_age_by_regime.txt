Prints the effective age by market state, Sections 9.1 and 10.2.

target SPX, 4852 test days, 2007-05-23 to 2026-09-03
    VIX files: ['VIX_History.csv']
VIX at t-1, terciles at 15.4 and 20.7; the two rungs are
percentiles of the series against its own trailing year, as in Table 13.

================================================================================================
A.  THE RATE AND THE AGE, IN EVERY STATE, ON THE SAME DAYS
================================================================================================
The rate divides by the damage delay does. The age does not divide by
anything: it asks how old a plain mark would have to be to forecast as
badly, by matching mean squared error on the same days.

               every day   4852    71.4%      4.6          0.4567   0.9432
   calm (bottom tercile)   1622    48.5%      4.5          0.4439   0.5679
          middle tercile   1615    52.3%      8.6          0.4517   0.7956
  stressed (top tercile)   1615    81.0%      3.8          0.4747   1.4677
             VIX top 10%    543    80.7%      3.4          0.7345   2.9783
              VIX top 5%    310    85.9%      2.3          0.7730   3.6926

  The rate and the age are computed from the same three quantities, so
  they are not independent readings. What differs is that one of them
  puts the delay damage in a denominator and the other does not.

  One row of that table was not what this file was built to find. Along
  the stress ladder the age falls monotonically: 3.8 days in the top
  tercile, 3.4 in the top decile, 2.3 in the top 5% of VIX days. On the days
  when a stale mark is most dangerous, a mark eleven weeks old plus
  today's foreign closes is worth a mark two days old.

================================================================================================
C.  IS THE CALM CELL UNDETERMINED, OR WAS IT THE RATIO?
================================================================================================
  calm     rate spans 100% of the unit interval; age spans 45.5 days
  stressed rate spans 18% of the unit interval; age spans 4.2 days

  relative width, calm against stressed: 5.5x on the rate, 9.2x on the age
  calm replications with no answer on the grid: 50 of 1500 (3.3%)

  A cell is read as determinate here when two things hold: fewer than
  10% of its replications fail to place the age on the grid, and its
  width relative to its own point is within 3x the stressed cell's.
  Calm: 3.3% fail to place, and 9.2x the stressed width.

================================================================================================
VERDICT
================================================================================================
  In calm markets the age is 4.5 days [0.8, 46.3], with 50 of 1500
  replications unable to place it on the grid at all. Changing
  coordinate does not rescue the calm cell.

  That is worth more than a number would have been. The calm-market
  non-result is not an artefact of putting the delay damage in a
  denominator: where delay costs a forecaster almost nothing, there is
  almost nothing for the cross-section to repair, and no coordinate
  makes a share of nothing precise. Section 11 can say that the
  limitation survives a change of instrument rather than resting on
  the one instrument that fails.
