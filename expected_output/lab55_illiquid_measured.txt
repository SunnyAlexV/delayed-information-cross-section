B.  THE PEER BLOCK, PRICED AS A SET AND CHOSEN BEFORE ANY RATE IS READ
==================================================================================================
  The selection statistic is S_cross(0), the cross arm's skill at ZERO
  delay, where the own mark is fresh and there is no substitution to
  detect. It cannot favour a high rate. Differences below
  0.01 are not material and ties break toward the SMALLER block,
  which is the direction that lowers the rate.

  S_own(0) does not depend on the peer block, so the bill each block pays
  is visible directly: a POSITIVE bill means the peer columns cost more
  than they earn even with nothing to substitute for. That is lab52's
  confound, and it contaminates every rate computed against that block.

  The three candidate blocks:
             every peer separately  each admissible peer enters as its own regressor
         peer mean (lab52's block)  one column: the equal-weighted mean of the peers, exactly the block lab52 defined
      peer mean + its 3-month mean  that mean and a three-month mean of it, two columns

==================================================================================================
VERDICT
==================================================================================================
  On fourteen metropolitan house price indices, monthly, 1987 to 2026,
  with no intraday range, no option chain, and a mark that is a
  three-month moving average before anyone touches it, the substitution
  rate at one quarterly appraisal cycle of staleness is 68%, interval
  [45%, 92%], positive on 14 of 14 metros.
  On the paper's OWN target type at this clock, 19% [6%, 31%], positive on 13 of 14.

  Both coordinates exclude zero, under the peer block a rate-blind rule
  selected and a conservative tie-break shrank. The paper's central
  claim is therefore not a fact about equity indices. It holds on an
  asset class with none of the machinery the equity results lean on,
  and it holds whether the target is the return an owner cares about or
  the variance the paper was written about.

  Part A's LEVEL prediction, checked in the form it was made rather than
  a looser one: coupling runs 0.662 to 0.866 here, at or near the top of
  the equity range, and the rate is positive on 14 of 14 metros on the
  return target and 13 of 14 on the volatility target, so it is
  confirmed on the return coordinate and holds with 1 exception on the
  volatility coordinate, named in Part D. The prediction was for no
  failures, so that exception is a partial miss and is recorded as one.
  All of this was stated before any rate existed.

  Three things the paper should stop saying. That the illiquid case is
  unreachable: it was reached. That the obstruction is the absence of
  data: the data was free and public throughout. That the frequency
  binds: lab54 showed it does not, and this file measures the rate at
  one twenty-first of the paper's sampling rate on marks an appraised
  asset could actually produce.

  Two things it should start saying. First, the measured persistence in
  Part A averages 0.804 and its minimum is 0.601, so every metro is more
  smoothed than the rho = 0.6 Section 13 imposed, and substitution works
  anyway. Second, and this is the load-bearing one: Part B shows the
  volatility rate is NEGATIVE at short delays under the widest peer
  block and positive under the compressed one. The estimation bill is
  not a footnote at this sample size, it is the difference between the
  paper's claim holding and appearing to fail, and lab52 found the same
  thing on equities. The paper should present compression as part of the
  method rather than as a robustness check.
