================================================================================================
0.  DOES THE TEST REJECT AT THE RATE IT PROMISES?
================================================================================================
Under a true null - every model a pure noise forecast of the same target -
a 5% test should reject about 5% of the time, and the Reality Check should
reject LESS often than SPA because it recentres hopeless models too.

  200 replications, 10 null models, 800 days each
  Reality Check rejects at 5%: 5.5%
  SPA rejects at 5%:           6.5%

  Sizes are usable.
  SPA rejects more often than the Reality Check, as Hansen predicts.
